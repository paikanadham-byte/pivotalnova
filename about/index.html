+About — The studio & the process

A studio that plots before it builds.

Pivotal Nova is a software development studio. No account managers, no hand-offs into the dark — you describe the business, we plot the spec, and the first version gets built before money is even discussed.

/ Who we are

Software for every type of brand

We build websites, web apps and mobile apps for businesses of every kind — the trades, hospitality, retail, services, and everything between. If your business runs on phone calls, paper and memory, we build the system that replaces the paper.

/ How we work

The risk sits with us, not you

Most software is bought blind: pay a deposit, hope the result matches the promise. We run it the other way round. We build your first version, you review it, and you only pay if you like it.

That single rule shapes everything else. Specs get plotted properly, because we are building for real from day one. Reviews are honest, because payment depends on them. And nobody is locked into a build they never wanted.

It also means we will tell you straight if something should not be built — a studio that only gets paid when a build is liked has no reason to sell you software you don’t need.

+Sec. 02 — Studio principles
/ P1 — Plot first

Spec before code

Every build starts as a written spec — pages, features, integrations — agreed before a line of code. No surprises mid-build.

/ P2 — Build first

Charge second

The first version is built before you pay anything. Our work has to earn the invoice, not the other way round.

/ P3 — Honest by default

No invented proof

We never fake reviews, stats or logos — not on this site, and not on yours. Proof is earned, then displayed.

+Sec. 03 — The process, plotted
Step / 01

Discover

We learn your business properly: what you sell, who it is for, how the day actually runs, and what the software has to do. This conversation becomes the spec — the drawing everything else is built from.

Output — A written spec you can check

Step / 02

First build

We build the first working version against that spec — the real design, the real features, your name on it. Not a mock-up, not a sales deck. A thing you can open, tap through and judge.

Output — A working first version, live for review

Step / 03

Review & refine

You review it properly — on your phone, on your desktop, with your customers in mind. We refine until it is right. This is the point where you decide: like it, and we proceed; don’t, and you owe nothing.

Output — Your decision, with zero risk taken

Step / 04

Launch & grow

The build goes live on your own domain. Then it keeps growing — refinements as your business changes, and SEO, AEO and GEO work so customers keep finding you in Google, in search and in AI answers.

Output — Live software that keeps earning
