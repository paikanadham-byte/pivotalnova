Questions

Asked, answered.

How does "build first, pay later" actually work?

Tell us what you need. We design and build the first version and send you a live link to open on your phone. You review it, ask for changes, and only pay if you decide to make it yours. If you don't, there's no bill and no hard feelings.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the build — a focused business website and a full web app are different animals. That's why there is no price list: after you've reviewed your first version, we quote one fixed price for your project, and you decide with the real thing in front of you.

Do I own my website or app?

Yes. Once you've paid for your project, the site, the app and your domain are yours. We'll always explain exactly what you're getting and what stays under your control.

Can you rebuild or improve my existing website?

Yes — most of our conversations start exactly there. We look at what you have, keep what works, and rebuild the rest. The build-first promise still applies: you see the new version before you pay.

Which countries do you work with?

We're a remote-first studio working mainly with clients in the UK, Canada and the US — and we're set up to work with businesses anywhere in the world.

What happens after launch?

We stay in your corner: updates, improvements and continued work on your visibility in Google, local search and AI answers, so the build keeps earning its keep long after launch day.
