+Sec. 04 — Questions, answered straight
How does build-first actually work?+
We talk through what your business needs (Discover), plot the spec, and build a real first version of your website or app (First build). You review it properly. If you like it, we refine, launch, and that is when you pay. If you don’t, you owe nothing.
What do you need from me to start?+
Just a conversation. Tell us about your business — what you sell, who it is for, and what you want the software to do. Email paikanadham@gmail.com or use the project form, and the spec starts there.
Can you build for my type of business?+
Yes — we build for every type of brand. Our concept builds alone cover twelve trades, from barbershops and roofing to cafés and tech repair; you can open them all on the Work page.
I already have a website. Can you still help?+
Tell us what you have and what it is failing to do. We build new websites, web apps and mobile apps — if a fresh build is the right route, we will spec it; the first version is still built before you pay.
Where are you based? Do you work in my country?+
We are a remote-first studio working with businesses in the UK, Canada and the US — and worldwide. Everything runs by email and video call, and your build is live on your own domain wherever you are.
What happens after launch?+
Launch is step four, not the finish line. Your site or app keeps growing — refinements as your business changes, and ongoing SEO, AEO and GEO work so customers keep finding you in Google, search and AI answers.
